# Privacy Policy — Screen Recorder

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

**App name:** Screen Recorder  
**Developer:** AKSByte Studio  
**Contact:** nextfusionapps@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

Screen Recorder is an Android application that allows users to record their device screen, capture supported audio, use a front-camera facecam, and manage recorded videos.

This policy explains how the app handles information and device permissions.

## 2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the app may process:

- Screen content captured after Android screen-capture permission is granted.
- Microphone or supported internal audio selected by the user.
- Front-camera video when facecam is enabled.
- Recording files, filenames, duration, file size, and related metadata.
- Local preferences such as video quality, audio source, facecam settings, watermark configuration, and save location.

Screen recordings, audio, and camera footage may contain sensitive information. Record only content you are authorized to capture.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The app processes screen, audio, and camera content to provide recording functionality. Recordings are saved to the device's configured storage location, including a custom folder selected by the user where supported.

The core recording functionality does not require an account.

## 4. Collection and Sharing

The app is designed for local recording and video management. Recordings are not intentionally uploaded to a developer-operated server as part of the core recording workflow.

When you choose to share a video, open it in another application, or upload it to a third-party service, that action may transfer the file to the selected recipient. The recipient's privacy practices then apply.

Third-party libraries and future app changes must be reviewed to determine whether additional collection or sharing disclosures are necessary.

## 5. Permissions

Depending on the features used, the app may request:

- **Screen capture:** To record screen content after Android's system consent prompt.
- **Microphone:** To record microphone audio when selected.
- **Camera:** To display the front-camera facecam.
- **Notifications:** To show recording status and controls.
- **Display over other apps:** To display supported floating controls and overlays.
- **Selected-folder access:** To save videos in a folder selected through Android's system folder picker.
- **Other applicable Android access:** To support implemented features such as touch-indicator guidance.

If permission is denied or revoked, the dependent feature may not work.

## 6. Storage and Retention

Recording files are stored on the device in the configured location. App preferences and recording-library information may also be stored locally.

Recordings generally remain until you or another process deletes them. You can use the app's video-management controls or your device's file manager to manage files.

Uninstalling the app may remove app-specific data, but recordings in shared storage may remain.

## 7. Security

The app relies on Android permissions and storage controls to restrict access to protected device features. No storage or processing method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated and protected.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility. Its intended audience and applicable distribution requirements should be reflected accurately in its Google Play declarations.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The app may use Android platform APIs and third-party software libraries. Their behavior and data practices should be reviewed against the actual released build.

If analytics, advertising, cloud storage, or other data-transmitting components are added, this policy must be updated accordingly.

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies, or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the public Privacy Policy URL provided for the app.
